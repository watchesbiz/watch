How this reference is built

Every number here carries a source tag. [STD] means it comes from a published standard such as ISO 764 or ISO 22810. [COMMON] means a widely repeated industry range that is directionally right but not a specification. [SYMPTOM] means you can verify it yourself with a phone and 24 hours. [JUDGEMENT] marks an editorial call, with the reasoning shown so you can overrule it.

Where the honest answer is "nobody has measured this," the page says so instead of inventing a figure — the running-versus-stopped question is the clearest example, and the page is written around that gap rather than over it.
